How it works

  • Your notes go to a Pollinations text model, which writes a short song: a hook that repeats and verses that carry one fact each.
  • The lyrics are set to music with a Pollinations music model, and if that model is not available on your account the same lyrics are spoken back instead, so you always get something to hear.
  • Quiz mode blanks the key words in your song, one click each, until you can sing it from memory.
  • Signing in is a plain OAuth + PKCE loop in your browser: your own Pollen pays for the writing and the music. Nothing is proxied.
